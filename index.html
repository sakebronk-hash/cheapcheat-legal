Privacy Policy

What it does

Add products you want to buy, record the prices you see, and set your own target price and deadline. Cheap Cheat shows the real price history and tells you whether to buy now or wait, always with the reason. You can share a product with someone else; you both see the same prices, and your own settings stay private.

Questions

Does Cheap Cheat check prices automatically?

No. Prices are entered by you and the people you share a product with. Tap “Check the price in the store” to open the shop.

How do I share a product?

Open the product → Share this watch → enter their email address. They need a Cheap Cheat account with that address and will see the invitation when they open the app.

How do I delete my account?

Info → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.
