Privacy Policy

Cheap Cheat · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Cheap Cheat is a wishlist that helps you decide when to buy. You add products, record the prices you see, and can share a product with people you invite. This page explains what that involves.

Your account

  • To use Cheap Cheat you create an account with your email address, a password and a display name.
  • Your email address is used to sign in, to confirm the address, to reset your password, and so that people can invite you to a shared product. Only you and people who invite you by that address can match it to you.
  • Your display name is shown to the people you share a product with.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.

What you store in Cheap Cheat

  • Products and prices: the products you add (name, brand, model, variant, shop, link) and the prices you record, with date, shop and who recorded them.
  • Your own settings per product: target price, purchase deadline, notes, notification choice and whether it's archived. These are private: nobody else can see them, also not the people you share a product with.
  • Sharing: who is part of a shared product, and open invitations (the invited email address and who invited them).
  • Photos you add to a product stay on your iPhone and are not uploaded.

Who can see what

  • A product you track, and its price history, is visible to you and to the people you share it with.
  • The price history belongs to the product at that shop. If someone else independently tracks exactly the same item at the same shop, the recorded prices of that item are shared with them, without names: they can't see who you are, your settings, or that you track it. A product without a shop item number or shop is never shared this way.
  • Nobody can browse or search other people's products or accounts.

Where your data is stored

Cheap Cheat uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as its processor. Your data is stored in Google's data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (region europe-west3), and transferred encrypted. Google processes it only to run the service. A copy of your data is kept on your iPhone so the app also works offline.

What Cheap Cheat does not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone else.
  • Cheap Cheat does not read prices from websites and does not send emails about you to anyone. Invitations are only shown inside the app to the invited address; you tell the other person yourself.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders about your purchase deadlines are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Your data is kept until you delete it or your account.
  • In the app (Info → Delete my account) you can delete your account at any time. This deletes your account, your profile, the products you own (also for the people you shared them with) and your personal settings, and removes you from products others shared with you. Prices you recorded in a shared price history stay, without your name or account.
  • You can also delete single products and prices you recorded, and export your data (Info → Export my data).

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by emailing us. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Austria: the Datenschutzbehörde).

Children

Cheap Cheat is not meant for children under 16 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Privacybeleid (samenvatting)

Voor Cheap Cheat maak je een account met e-mailadres, wachtwoord en weergavenaam. Je producten, de prijzen die je vastlegt en je eigen instellingen (doelprijs, deadline, notities, meldingen) worden bewaard in Google Firebase in Frankfurt. Je eigen instellingen ziet niemand anders. Een product en de prijsgeschiedenis zien jij en de mensen die je uitnodigt; prijzen van precies hetzelfde artikel bij dezelfde winkel worden zonder naam gedeeld. Foto's blijven op je iPhone. Geen advertenties, geen tracking, geen verkoop van gegevens. In de app kun je je account en gegevens op elk moment verwijderen. Contact: bas@sebastianbronk.com.

Datenschutzerklärung (Zusammenfassung)

Für Cheap Cheat erstellst du ein Konto mit E-Mail-Adresse, Passwort und Anzeigenamen. Deine Produkte, die von dir erfassten Preise und deine eigenen Einstellungen (Zielpreis, Frist, Notizen, Mitteilungen) werden bei Google Firebase in Frankfurt gespeichert. Deine eigenen Einstellungen sieht niemand sonst. Ein Produkt und seinen Preisverlauf sehen du und die Personen, die du einlädst; Preise genau desselben Artikels im selben Shop werden ohne Namen geteilt. Fotos bleiben auf deinem iPhone. Keine Werbung, kein Tracking, kein Verkauf von Daten. In der App kannst du dein Konto und deine Daten jederzeit löschen. Kontakt: bas@sebastianbronk.com.
